# Contributing

Thanks for helping.

## How work happens

- Pick an open issue, branch from `main` (`feat/<issue-number>-short-name`), open a pull request that closes the issue.
- CI must be green before merge. Keep PRs focused on one issue.

## Style

- Plain Australian English (organisation, licence, analyse).
- No em dashes and no emojis.
- Every factual claim about a Microsoft product or ASD publication links to the official source (Microsoft Learn, cyber.gov.au).
- Do not claim or imply endorsement by ASD, ACSC or Microsoft.
- No real tenant ids, subscription ids, secrets or customer data. Use placeholders such as `00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000`.
